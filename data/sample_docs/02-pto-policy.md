# Time Off and Leave Policy

_Fictional HR policy for Vision AI Ops employees. Portfolio sample only._

## PTO days

Full-time employees receive **20 PTO days** of paid time off each calendar year, accrued monthly. Up to **5 unused PTO days** may carry into the next year; the rest expire on 31 December.

## Requesting time off

1. Submit the request in the People inbox at least **5 business days** ahead for absences of 1–3 days.
2. Absences of 4 or more days need **10 business days** notice unless the leave is medical or legally protected.
3. Managers respond within 2 business days. PTO is not denied solely because a sprint is in progress.

## Sick leave

Employees receive **8 sick days** per year, separate from PTO. Do not come to the office or join customer calls with a fever. For three or more consecutive sick days, email People with a brief status; a doctor's note is only required after **5 consecutive calendar days**.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive **12 weeks** of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive **4 weeks**. Leave may start up to 2 weeks before a due date or adoption placement.

## Company holidays (US)

New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, the Friday after Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day. Floating cultural holidays: 2 additional days chosen by the employee each year.
