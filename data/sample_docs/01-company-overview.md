# Vision AI Ops — Company Overview

_Fictional company profile for a portfolio RAG demo. Not a real business. No production credentials._

Vision AI Ops is a small product studio that helps operations teams see what their AI systems are doing in production. The flagship product is **TraceLight**, an internal tracing and eval workspace. A companion alerting layer, **AlertMesh**, routes quality regressions to Slack-compatible webhooks that the customer already owns.

## What we sell

- TraceLight Cloud — hosted workspace for traces, spans, and eval scores
- TraceLight Local — Docker compose stack for teams that cannot send prompts off-network
- AlertMesh — threshold and evaluator-driven alerts (quality, latency, cost)

We do **not** resell iPaaS or RPA platforms. Integrations in this demo are generic HTTP webhooks and a local FastAPI assistant.

## Who we serve

Mid-size software teams (50–400 people) that already run an LLM feature and need a shared place for traces, prompts, and human review. Typical buyers: Head of Engineering, ML platform lead, or Support operations manager.

## Support snapshot

- Support hours: Monday–Friday 09:00–18:00 US/Eastern
- P1 (production outage of TraceLight ingest): first response in 30 minutes
- P2 (degraded evaluator runs): first response in 4 business hours
- Data residency for TraceLight Cloud: US-East or EU-West, chosen at workspace create time
