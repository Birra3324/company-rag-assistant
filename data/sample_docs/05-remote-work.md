# Remote Work and Expenses

_Fictional workplace policy for Vision AI Ops. Portfolio sample only._

Vision AI Ops is **hybrid**. Employees within 50 miles of the Cambridge listing are asked to be on-site **Tuesdays and Thursdays**. Everyone else is remote-first. Core overlap hours are **11:00–16:00 US/Eastern** for customer-facing roles.

## Home office stipend

Full-time employees receive **$150 per month** for internet and desk setup. Submit receipts monthly in the expenses tool. The stipend does not cover coffee, gym memberships, or personal SaaS.

## Travel

Customer on-sites require manager approval when the estimated trip cost exceeds **$800**. Book refundable fares when the meeting is not confirmed. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Equipment

New hires receive a 14-inch laptop and an external keyboard. Lost chargers are replaced once per year at company cost; a second replacement is a personal expense.

## Time zones

The support rotation covers US/Eastern first. EU-based teammates can own EU-West workspace tickets during local morning hours. Overnight P1 coverage is a paid on-call stipend of **$75 per weeknight**, described in the People handbook (not ingested here yet).
